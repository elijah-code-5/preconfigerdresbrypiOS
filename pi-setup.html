Pi Hub setup

Fill this in, press the button, and you get two small files. Put them on the Raspberry Pi's memory card and the Pi sets itself up. Nothing you type leaves this page.

1. Your details

2. Download the two files

The names must be exactly user-data and network-config, with no .txt at the end.

3. Flash and finish

  1. Open Raspberry Pi Imager. Choose Raspberry Pi 4 and Raspberry Pi OS (64-bit) (the one with the desktop), then your memory card.
  2. If Imager offers to customise settings, choose No / Skip. Write the card.
  3. If the card disappears when Imager finishes, unplug it and plug it back in. A drive called bootfs appears.
  4. Copy the two files onto bootfs and say yes to replace the ones already there.
  5. Put the card in the Pi, connect the screen, and power on. Leave it alone for 15 to 30 minutes. It needs internet and restarts by itself once. Then the desktop appears with a window showing the Pi's IP address and logins.
